Kenosian builds cryptographic timing and integrity protocols for data centers, AI inference infrastructure, and critical network systems. Enterprise access only.

Protocol

TTTPS (Temporal Trust Timestamping Protocol Suite) provides tamper-evident, multi-source verified timestamps for enterprise systems. Designed for Byzantine adversarial environments — each timestamp is independently auditable and carries cryptographic proof of issuance time and data integrity.

Deployed across AI inference infrastructure, CDN networks, and grid management systems. IETF standardization in progress.
